## Notes

1. **Location Assignment**: All items should be assigned to the `new-orleans` location taxonomy term
2. **Tiered Pricing**: The "Request Specific DJ" and "Request Specific Photographer" add-ons use tiered pricing (options with different prices). This may require custom handling in the admin UI.
3. **Extras Field**: The "Cold Spark Fountains" add-on has an `extras` field: `{ "Blast": 200 }`
4. **No Coordination Service**: New Orleans does NOT have a Coordination service (unlike Austin)
