## Summary

- **Services**: 4 services (all shared with Austin, but different pricing/packages)
- **Packages**: 8 packages total
  - Different package names/structures than Austin
  - 1 Washington DC-exclusive package (Diamond Combo DJ Package with bundled Photo Booth)
  - Photography packages are numbered (Package 1-4)
  - Videography packages are lettered (Package A-C)
  - Photo Booth has only 1 package (vs multiple in other locations)
- **Add-ons**: 22 add-ons total
  - Most shared with Austin (different prices)
  - 7 Washington DC-exclusive add-ons:
    1. Add Ceremony Sound Package
    2. Dancing on a Cloud
    3. Dance Floor Lighting (2 Moving Heads on Totems)
    4. Audio Guestbook Phone
    5. Special Reservation (DJ/MC)
    6. Expedited Editing
    7. Raw Footage (Before Event)
    8. Raw Footage (After Event, If Available)
  - 1 add-on with options (Engagement or Bridal Session)
  - 1 add-on with extras (Cold Spark Fountains)

## Key Differences from Austin

1. **Package Structure**: 
   - DJ packages use "Gold", "Platinum", "Diamond" naming vs Austin's named packages
   - Photography uses numbered packages (Package 1-4) vs named packages
   - Videography uses lettered packages (Package A-C) vs named packages
   - Photo Booth has only 1 package vs multiple packages in Austin

2. **Unique Features**:
   - "Free Travel Within 30 Miles of DC" included in Gold DJ Package and Package A
   - Diamond Combo DJ Package bundles Photo Booth service
   - Several unique add-ons not available in Austin

3. **Pricing Differences**:
   - Extra Hour for DJ/MC: $325/hour (vs $300/hour in Austin)
   - Uplighting: Fixed $395 for 10 lights (vs per-unit pricing in Austin)
   - Various other price differences throughout

## Notes

1. **Location Assignment**: All items should be assigned to the `washington` location taxonomy term
2. **Bundled Services**: The Diamond Combo DJ Package includes the Ultimate Photo Booth as a bundled service
3. **Extras Field**: The "Cold Spark Fountains" add-on has an `extras` field: `{ "4 Sparks": 200, "Additional Uses": 200 }`
4. **Options Field**: The "Engagement or Bridal Session" add-on has options: ["Engagement Session", "Bridal Session", "Both"]
5. **No Coordination Service**: Washington DC does NOT have a Coordination service (unlike Austin)
